Theme-relative colour tokens are wrong on a surface whose background never follows the theme. Two instances in AnEntrypoint/design: the .cli card sets background var(--ink) in EVERY theme, so its --fg-3 text resolved to the paper tier under light and measured 2.48:1; and preview swatches painting a constant fill used color:var(--panel-0), measuring 1.15:1 on ink. Both need fixed tones (--ink/--paper/--paper-3-dark), not ramp tokens. Related and more general: --accent is the FILL and --accent-ink the readable TEXT tone -- bare --accent as text measures 1.07:1 on paper while being identical to --accent-ink in dark, which is exactly why this bug class survives review until someone opens the other theme.
