Committed PNG baselines cannot gate across platforms. In AnEntrypoint/design a full re-capture on Windows drifted 78 of 81 pages on an Ubuntu runner -- every failing page a type specimen at 0.7-2.5% with max channel delta ~230 and no layout change, i.e. font rasterization. No tolerance separates that from a real single-element shift: raising it far enough to absorb rasterization also absorbs what the gate exists to catch. The honest resolution is report-only cross-platform with the reasoning recorded in the workflow, and capturing baselines in a container matching the runner as the real fix.
