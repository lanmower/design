prefers-color-scheme defaults differ per machine, which silently makes local and CI audit different themes. In AnEntrypoint/design every kit ships data-theme=auto; the local headless Chrome defaulted to dark while the ubuntu-latest runner defaulted to light, so an axe-core a11y gate reported 0 locally and 8 in CI, and a pixel-diff harness flapped on 38 of 78 baselines. Both were fixed by pinning Emulation.setEmulatedMedia (prefers-color-scheme AND prefers-reduced-motion) before navigation -- the motion pin also stopped axe compositing mid-transition colours, which had made one kit alternate between 0 and 30 violations on settle timing. Fix the environment determinism, never the baseline.
