🔤 Responsive Typography

Headings use clamp() for fluid scaling across breakpoints.

Heading Level 1

font-size: clamp(34px, 5cqi, 64px)

Heading Level 2

font-size: clamp(28px, 3.6cqi, 44px)

Heading Level 3

font-size: clamp(22px, 2.4cqi, 30px)

Body text remains readable at all breakpoints. On mobile, it's 14px. On tablet, 16px. On desktop, 16px with adjusted line-height.
