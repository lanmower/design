.ds-panel-duo — a pair of different things, top-aligned
traffic by channel4
Deliberately NOT stretched. A duo pairs two unlike things — a four-bar chart beside a five-row table — and forcing a common height does not create equality, it just moves the shorter panel's dead space inside its own body.
top endpoints5
Each takes its natural height and they align at the top, where the eye actually starts.
.ds-panel-gap — outer rhythm, 2:1 against the inner sibling gap
first
Outer separation is --space-5 and the gap between siblings inside a panel body is --space-3, both density-scaled. The ratio is what matters: if outer ever drops below inner, proximity starts claiming that two unrelated panels belong together more tightly than two rows inside one panel.
second
Set data-density to compact or spacious on the root and both intervals move together, holding the ratio.
