Panel rows

Resize the window. These step on CONTAINER width, not viewport width, so they reflow when the panels are actually cramped rather than at a number that ignores how much chrome the shell is holding. Trio steps 3 → 2 → 1; duo steps 2 → 1.

.ds-panel-duo — a pair of different things, top-aligned
traffic by channel4
Deliberately NOT stretched. A duo pairs two unlike things — a four-bar chart beside a five-row table — and forcing a common height does not create equality, it just moves the shorter panel's dead space inside its own body.
top endpoints5
Each takes its natural height and they align at the top, where the eye actually starts.
.ds-panel-gap — outer rhythm, 2:1 against the inner sibling gap
first
Outer separation is --space-5 and the gap between siblings inside a panel body is --space-3, both density-scaled. The ratio is what matters: if outer ever drops below inner, proximity starts claiming that two unrelated panels belong together more tightly than two rows inside one panel.
second
Set data-density to compact or spacious on the root and both intervals move together, holding the ratio.
panel-on-panel — nesting depth limit
nesting depth
The token ladder only defines two panel surfaces above the page background: --panel-1 (--bg-2) and --panel-2 (--bg-3). That is a hard, deliberate ceiling of 2 levels of nesting (page → panel → panel-in-panel) — there is no --panel-3 step. A third nested level has nothing left to escalate to, so it either repeats --panel-2 (the nested panel becomes visually indistinguishable from its parent, and the hierarchy reads as flat) or falls back to a border-only treatment. Depth 3+ is where the pattern breaks down: if content needs a third visually distinct layer, it should not be another nested panel — use a modal/drawer/dialog surface instead, which resets the depth stack rather than extending it.
DEPTH 1 — --panel-1 / --bg-2
DEPTH 2 — --panel-2 / --bg-3, still legible against depth 1
DEPTH 3 — no --panel-3 token exists; this repeats --panel-2 and is visually indistinguishable from its parent. This is the breakdown point — stop nesting panels here and use a modal/drawer instead.
